Poesie Simona Clinae is a book of poems you don't read so much as watch flow by.

• Stream: stanzas from all poems drift by in random order, on their own
• Poem: one poem, stanza by stanza, at a calm pace
• Reading: a whole poem at once; swipe from poem to poem in an endless loop
• Two collections are free, two more are one-time purchases, no subscription
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking

The poems are in Czech and are not translated; the app interface is available in 45 languages. The poems speak openly about the body, sexuality, illness, death and faith and use coarse language in places.
