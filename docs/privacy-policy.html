Privacy Policy

Poesie Simona Clinae

This Privacy Policy describes how Olin Vorechovsky / A+B (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile application Poesie Simona Clinae (the “App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Effective date: April 21, 2026

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App operates without user accounts, registration, or login.

All data created within the App (your settings and any poems you add manually) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional paid content through Google Play’s in-app purchase system. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store any payment card or billing information. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles purchase data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — The App downloads typefaces from Google Fonts servers at runtime. Google may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent). See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — Used to process in-app purchases. Governed by Google’s policies.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Advertising ID

The App does not use the Android Advertising ID or any equivalent identifier for advertising purposes.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
